## 📊 Scoring Framework

### Scoring Criteria

## 📋 Platform Analysis

### Layer3 - Speed Champion

**Overall Score: 9.0/10**

**Strengths:**
- ✅ Fastest payout architecture (instant)
- ✅ Lowest effort (complete action, claim reward)
- ✅ High reliability for eligible completions

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ Small reward sizes ($0.10-$5)
- ❌ Limited upside (campaign caps)
- ❌ Not sustainable as primary income

**Best Use Case:**
> Daily micro-earning while building reputation on other platforms

**Evidence:**
> Layer3 Rewards FAQ: "Liquid Rewards flow directly to your wallet right after completion"

---

### LaborX - Reliability Champion

**Overall Score: 7.8/10**

**Strengths:**
- ✅ Highest payment certainty (escrow)
- ✅ Real paid work (not micro-rewards)
- ✅ Good upside potential

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ Not instant (client acceptance)
- ❌ Higher effort (real work)
- ❌ Need to win contracts first

**Best Use Case:**
> Primary income channel for dependable crypto earnings

**Evidence:**
> LaborX: "Funds are escrowed in a smart contract and automatically released when work is completed and accepted"

---

### Galxe - Balanced Option

**Overall Score: 7.2/10**

**Strengths:**
- ✅ Fast claim flows (when live)
- ✅ Low-medium effort
- ✅ Campaign variety

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ Inconsistent timing by campaign
- ❌ Some rewards are raffle-based
- ❌ Claim fees can apply

**Best Use Case:**
> Secondary channel alongside Layer3 for quest diversity

**Evidence:**
> Galxe Earndrop docs: Token distribution with one-time or vesting models

---

### Gitcoin Bounties - Structured Work

**Overall Score: 5.8/10**

**Strengths:**
- ✅ Clear task/reward structure
- ✅ Open-source reputation building
- ✅ Medium upside

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ Approval-gated settlement
- ❌ Maintainer timing varies
- ❌ Not optimized for speed

**Best Use Case:**
> Long-term open-source career building

**Evidence:**
> Gitcoin: Bounties paid in ETH/USDC to wallet upon approval

---

### Superteam Earn - Solana Focus

**Overall Score: 5.6/10**

**Strengths:**
- ✅ High opportunity density
- ✅ Solana ecosystem focus
- ✅ Multiple categories (dev, design, content)

**Weaknesses:**
- ❌ Competitive (not guaranteed)
- ❌ Sponsor review delays
- ❌ Variable payout timing

**Best Use Case:**
> Solana-specific bounty hunting

**Evidence:**
> Superteam FAQ: "Sponsors typically review submissions and select winners"

## 📊 Methodology Notes

### Scoring Calculation

**Overall Score** = (Speed × 0.40) + (Reliability × 0.30) + ((10 - Effort) × 0.15) + (Upside × 0.15)

**Example - Layer3:**
- Speed: 10 × 0.40 = 4.0
- Reliability: 7 × 0.30 = 2.1
- Effort: (10-3) × 0.15 = 1.05
- Upside: 3 × 0.15 = 0.45
- **Total: 9.0**

### Data Quality

- Scores based on official platform documentation
- User reports and community feedback
- Direct testing where possible
- Some scores inferential (Gitcoin primary source not verified in final pass)
